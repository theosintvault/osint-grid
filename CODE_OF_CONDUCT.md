# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment
We are committed to fostering an open, welcoming, and professional environment within The OSINT Grid project and its community.

## Standards
Participants are expected to:
- Use respectful and inclusive language
- Provide constructive feedback
- Accept responsibility for their actions
- Maintain professionalism in all interactions

Unacceptable behavior includes:
- Harassment or discrimination
- Personal attacks or insults
- Publishing private information without consent
- Disruptive or hostile conduct

## Enforcement
Violations may be reported to:
conduct@theosintvault.io

All reports will be reviewed and addressed promptly and confidentially.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies to:
- GitHub issues and pull requests
- Discussions and community spaces
- Any interactions related to The OSINT Grid project
