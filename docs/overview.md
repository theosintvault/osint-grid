# OSINT Grid Overview

The OSINT Grid is a structured index of 4,500+ verified public records sources curated by The OSINT Vault. It provides investigators, analysts, journalists, compliance teams, and intelligence professionals with a centralized directory of authoritative public‑records systems across corporate, property, court, licensing, and government domains.

## What the OSINT Grid Provides
- A unified directory of public‑records sources across federal, state, county, municipal, and international jurisdictions  
- Categorized and metadata‑rich entries for fast filtering and investigative pivoting  
- Verified, high‑integrity sources suitable for due diligence, fraud analysis, asset tracing, and regulatory workflows  
- A continuously maintained dataset aligned with real‑world investigative needs  

## Categories Included
- Corporate & Business Registries  
- Property & Land Records  
- Court & Case Lookup Systems  
- Licensing & Permits  
- Government Directories  
- Public Databases  
- International Records
